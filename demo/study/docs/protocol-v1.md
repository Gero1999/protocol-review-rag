# Synthetic study protocol, version 1

## Applicability and scope

This fictional parallel-group study examines daily routines in adults.
This protocol applies to participants assigned version 1 in the study
mapping. The mapping also identifies the participants expected at Visit 3.
Each listed participant is expected to have one planned Visit 3 for review.

## Visit 3 timing

Count calendar days from randomization, which is day 0. Schedule Visit 3
on day 14, with an allowable window from day 12 through day 16, inclusive.
Use the observed visit start date to assess timing.

## Visit 3 blood pressure

Record both systolic and diastolic blood pressure at Visit 3. Keep the
two results separate so that either missing component can be identified
during study review.

| Scheduled visit | Required measurements |
| --- | --- |
| Visit 3 | Systolic and diastolic blood pressure |

## Visit 3 chemistry

Record both serum glucose and serum creatinine results for Visit 3.
An assessment or result from another visit does not fulfill this schedule.

| Scheduled visit | Required laboratory results |
| --- | --- |
| Visit 3 | Glucose and creatinine |
