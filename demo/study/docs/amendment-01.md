# Synthetic study amendment 01

## Visit 3 timing amendment

The timing window moves one day later, from days 12–16 in version 1 to
days 13–17 in version 2. The limits are inclusive and randomization
remains day 0. This allows the study team to schedule Visit 3 on a later
day without removing either expected assessment.

| Protocol | Target day | Earliest day | Latest day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Version 1 | 14 | 12 | 16 |
| Version 2 | 15 | 13 | 17 |

## Version applicability

Use the protocol version declared for each participant in the study mapping.
An observed Visit 3 date alone cannot establish which version governs.
Obtain the missing assignment before making a version-specific finding.
