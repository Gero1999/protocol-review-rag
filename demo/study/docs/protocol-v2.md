# Synthetic study protocol, version 2

## Applicability and scope

This revision applies to participants assigned version 2 in the study
mapping. Use the version 1 protocol for participants whose mapping declares
version 1. If no version is assigned, the applicable requirements cannot be
confirmed from this packet.

## Visit 3 timing

Count calendar days from randomization, which is day 0. Schedule Visit 3
on day 15, with an allowable window from day 13 through day 17, inclusive.
Use the observed visit start date to assess timing.

## Visit 3 blood pressure

Record both systolic and diastolic blood pressure at Visit 3. The timing
amendment leaves this assessment unchanged.

| Scheduled visit | Required measurements |
| --- | --- |
| Visit 3 | Systolic and diastolic blood pressure |

## Visit 3 chemistry

Record both serum glucose and serum creatinine results for Visit 3.
An assessment or result from another visit does not fulfill this schedule.

| Scheduled visit | Required laboratory results |
| --- | --- |
| Visit 3 | Glucose and creatinine |
